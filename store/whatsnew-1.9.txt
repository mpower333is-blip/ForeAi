APPLE APP STORE — "What's New in This Version"  (max 4000 characters)
────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
What's new in 1.9

⛳ Score the whole four-ball from one phone
Pick who you're playing with, mark your team, and card everyone as you go — your
scores and your partners' post straight to the live golf-day leaderboard.
Betterball? Your team shows on top, the other pair underneath.

🏆 Prize-giving
See the day's winners right on your phone: overall, best gross, handicap
divisions, front/back nines, the two's club, team prizes and the side games
(nearest the pin, longest drive).

✨ Smoother golf days
• The app finds you automatically on an event's player list — no more picking
  your name every time.
• Finish a hole and it moves to the next one for you.
• A quick "save your card?" prompt at the turn and at the 18th.
• Your round saves as you play, so nothing's lost if the app closes.

📱 Built for every screen
Fills the full display on foldables (unfolded) and tablets, with a cleaner
scoring layout and easier-to-read GPS distances.

🔧 Reliability
Fixes a crash that could stop the app from opening on launch for some players,
plus a range of under-the-hood improvements.

APP STORE — reviewer notes (request EXPEDITED review; paste under "Notes")
────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
This update fixes a critical launch crash in the currently-live version (1.8):
on newer iOS versions the app crashes immediately on startup with a Firebase
authentication component registration error, so users cannot open it at all.
Version 1.9 resolves the crash. We're requesting expedited review so affected
users can update as soon as possible. No account or login is needed to open the
app or reproduce — it launches straight into the golf features.


Notes
-----
- The same copy works for both stores; use the short Google Play version where
  Play asks for release notes.
- The live-1.8 launch crash is fixed by the APP CODE in 1.9 (Metro/Firebase
  bundling + startup guards), not by the Firebase console. Enabling Anonymous
  sign-in in Firebase was a separate fix (it lets scores save) and does not stop
  the 1.8 binary from crashing — only shipping 1.9 does.
