## ⚠️ One review risk to know about

Apple's App Review Guideline **4.2 (minimum functionality)** / **4.3 (spam)** can
flag apps that look tied to a single event or too thin. This app has real,
year-round utility (GPS, swing coach, caddie, scoring), so keep the listing
framed as a **reusable golf-day companion**, not "an app for one day." The copy
above already does this. If Apple pushes back, the fastest answer is: it's a
general golf-day/tournament companion that happens to ship pre-loaded for the
ECS Golf Day — same reasoning that gets society/club apps approved.
