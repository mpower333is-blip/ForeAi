**Description** (max 4000)
```
ForeAi is your AI golf caddie — everything you need to play smarter, lower-scoring golf, in one beautifully simple app.

PLAY A GOLF DAY — FREE
Joining an event or fundraiser? Enter the code, tap your name, and you're in. Score your whole four-ball live, follow the leaderboard, see each team move around the course, and get GPS distances to every green — all free on the day, with the full app unlocked so you can try everything.

YOUR AI CADDIE
Smart club calls based on your distance, the lie and the wind — and clubs that learn your real carry distances as you play, so every round the advice gets sharper. A caddie that actually knows your bag.

GPS TO EVERY GREEN
Front, middle and back distances the moment you're on the hole. No rangefinder needed.

SWING COACH IN YOUR POCKET
Film your swing and get instant, easy feedback on tempo and posture — practice with a plan.

TRACK EVERY ROUND
Live scorecard, strokes-gained stats and trends, and a course-strategy plan for every hole — see exactly where you win and lose shots.

RUN A GOLF DAY
Organising a society day or fundraiser? Create the event, share a join code, add sponsors and prizes, and watch the scores roll in live — with a sponsor thank-you wall to celebrate everyone who helped.

FOREAI PRO
Loved the golf day and want ForeAi for your own rounds? Subscribe to ForeAi Pro — monthly or annual — to keep the full toolkit: live round tracking, the learning AI caddie, GPS rangefinder, unlimited Swing Coach, strokes-gained stats and course strategy.

Free to download and free for the golf day. ForeAi Pro is an auto-renewing subscription; cancel anytime.

Questions? foreai.co.za
```
