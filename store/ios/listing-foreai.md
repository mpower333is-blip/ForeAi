ForeAi is your AI golf caddie — everything you need to play smarter, lower-scoring golf, in one beautifully simple app.

WHAT YOU GET FREE
• AI Swing Coach — film your swing and get instant, easy feedback on tempo and posture.
• AI Caddie (demo) — smart club suggestions based on your distance to the target.
• Golf Days & Events — join or run a golf day, share a code, and follow a live leaderboard.

GO PRO
Unlock the full on-course experience with a one-time in-app purchase:
• Live Round — GPS distances to the front, middle and back of every green. No rangefinder needed.

• Course Strategy — a plan for every hole, tuned to your game.
• Golf Games — Skins, Stableford, Match Play and more with your mates.

CLUBS THAT LEARN YOU
ForeAi learns your real carry distances as you play, so the club calls get more accurate every round — a caddie that actually knows your bag.

RUN A GOLF DAY
Organising a fundraiser or society day? Create an event, hand out a join code, add sponsors and prizes, and watch the scores roll in live on the clubhouse board.
