ForeAi 2.0 — Release notes
==========================

2.0 carries the golf-day scoring + prize-giving work, the new club tracking and
golf-format guide, and the fix for the Google Play prominent-disclosure policy
flag (an in-app explanation of how location is used now precedes the permission
request). versionName applies to both stores; Android versionCode auto-increments
from the CI build number.

APPLE APP STORE — "What's New in This Version"  (max 4000 characters)
────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
What's new in 2.0

⛳ Score the whole four-ball from one phone
Pick who you're playing with, mark your team, and card everyone as you go — your
scores and your partners' post straight to the live golf-day leaderboard.
Betterball? Your team shows on top, the other pair underneath.

📋 Know how the day is played
Every event now shows how it's played and how it's scored — scramble, betterball,
foursomes, Stableford and more — so nobody's guessing the rules on the first tee.

🎒 My Bag — see what new clubs do for you
Save the make and model of each club. Swap in a new driver and ForeAi keeps your
old club's numbers, then shows the difference the new one makes to your real carry
distance as you log shots.

🏆 Prize-giving
See the day's winners right on your phone: overall, best gross, handicap divisions,
front/back nines, the two's club, team prizes and the side games.

✨ Smoother golf days
• The app finds you automatically on an event's player list — no more picking your
  name every time.
• Finish a hole and it moves to the next one for you, with a quick "save your card?"
  prompt at the turn and at the 18th.
• Your round saves as you play, so nothing's lost if the app closes.

📱 Built for every screen
Fills the full display on foldables (unfolded) and tablets, with a cleaner scoring
layout and easier-to-read GPS distances.

🔧 Reliability & privacy
A clearer in-app explanation of how ForeAi uses your location (course distances,
weather and sharing your position with the golf-day leaderboard) now appears before
we ask for permission, plus a range of under-the-hood fixes.


────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
GOOGLE PLAY — policy reviewer notes (paste when resubmitting)
────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
This build resolves the "Prominent Disclosure and Consent Requirement:
Inadequate Prominent Disclosure" flag under the User Data policy.

Before requesting the foreground location runtime permission, the app now shows
an in-app prominent disclosure that names the data (device location) and how it
is used: on-course GPS distances and hole maps, local weather, and — during a
golf day the player has joined — sharing the player's live position with the
event organiser's clubhouse leaderboard. The OS permission prompt is only
requested after the player accepts this disclosure. This mirrors the existing
background-location disclosure already used for the lightning-safety feature.

The Play Data safety form declares Location as collected and shared (for the
live leaderboard), matching the in-app disclosure text.
