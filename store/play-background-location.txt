ForeAi — Google Play background-location declaration & demo video
=================================================================

Why this file exists
--------------------
Google rejected the 2.0 upload for "Inadequate Prominent Disclosure" (Location).
The app code now shows an in-app prominent disclosure that covers background
location (fixed in v2.1). To KEEP the lightning-safety feature that runs when the
app is closed, Google ALSO requires you to (a) declare background location in the
Play Console and (b) attach a short demo video. This file has the exact wording
and a step-by-step shot list so you can film it in one take.

Package: com.foreai.mobile   ·   App: ForeAi: Golf GPS Rangefinder
Feature needing background location: lightning-strike safety warnings during a round.


────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
1. BACKGROUND LOCATION DECLARATION  (Play Console)
   App content → Sensitive app permissions → Location  (or the
   "Permissions declaration form" shown with the policy notice)
────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

• Does your app use background location? → YES

• Which feature uses it? (paste)
  Lightning-strike safety warnings. During a round ForeAi checks the player's
  location against live lightning data and sends a loud warning to take shelter
  when a strike is detected nearby.

• Why does the feature need location in the BACKGROUND / when the app is closed?
  (paste)
  Golfers keep their phone in a pocket or bag with the screen off and the app not
  in the foreground for most of a 4–5 hour round. A lightning warning is only
  useful if it still fires while the app is closed — a safety alert the player
  cannot see is no protection. The watch runs ONLY during an active round and
  stops automatically when the round ends; it is not used at any other time.

• Is background access limited to what the feature needs? → YES
  It runs only during an active round, uses balanced accuracy, and stops at the
  end of the round.

• Video link: <paste the unlisted YouTube link from section 2>


────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
2. DEMO VIDEO  (screen recording of the phone; upload to YouTube as UNLISTED,
   then paste the link into the declaration above)
────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

Keep it ~60–90 seconds. Narrate or add captions so the reviewer follows along.
Film on the v2.1 build (the one with the updated disclosure).

Shot list — record in this order, without cutting:

  1. Fresh start: open ForeAi (ideally a fresh install so the disclosure shows).
     Tap through the short intro to the Home screen.

  2. PROMINENT DISCLOSURE — the dialog titled "ForeAi uses your location" appears.
     Pause ~3 seconds on it so the reviewer can read it (it names GPS distances,
     weather, sharing your position with the golf-day leaderboard, AND the
     lightning safety watch that keeps checking location in the background even
     when the app is closed). Then tap ALLOW.

  3. System location prompt appears → choose "While using the app".

  4. Turn the lightning safety feature on (if not already): Profile →
     Notifications → "⚡ Lightning alerts" ON. (Or start/join a round so the
     round lightning watch starts.)

  5. BACKGROUND DISCLOSURE + permission — when the background watch starts, the
     app shows its "Lightning safety uses your location" dialog (it says location
     is used in the background and when the app is closed, only during a round).
     Tap Allow → on the next system screen choose "Allow all the time".

  6. Show the lightning status on Home (e.g. "Skies clear — no lightning within
     10 km") so the feature is visibly working.

  7. Press the phone's Home button to background/close the app, wait a couple of
     seconds, to show the watch is designed to keep running while the app is not
     open. (You don't need a real strike — just show the app backgrounded.)

Tip: Android has a built-in screen recorder (swipe down → Screen record). Record,
then upload to YouTube set to "Unlisted" and copy the share link.

3. DATA SAFETY FORM  (App content → Data safety) — must match the disclosure
────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

• Location → Approximate location AND Precise location: COLLECTED.
• Collected in the background: YES.
• Shared: YES — the player's position is shared with the golf-day organiser's
  clubhouse leaderboard during an event.
• Purposes: App functionality (on-course distances, hole maps), and the live
  golf-day leaderboard. NOT used for advertising or marketing.

────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
If it's rejected AGAIN
────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
Google holds background location to a "core feature" bar, and a lightning add-on
can still be refused even with a perfect disclosure + video. If that happens, the
two clean fixes are:
  • remove background location — lightning then works only while the app is open
    (no declaration/video needed, fastest approval); or
  • move lightning to push notifications — server sends the warning, no on-device
    background location (needs backend work).
Ask and either can be done quickly.
