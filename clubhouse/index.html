What we do

Golf technology, end to end.

ForeAi is a South African golf-tech company. We build one app for every golfer, the tools clubs use to run their golf, and the programmes that bring the game to kids who'd never otherwise get to play.

📱

The app

GPS distances, satellite hole views, an AI caddie and swing coach, live round tracking and strokes-gained stats — on your phone and Apple Watch.

🏌️

Golf days & clubs

Run a golf day end to end — registration, live scoring, a big-screen leaderboard and a live course map — plus club apps for members, competitions and news.

💚

Golf for all

School leagues and affiliation for children who can't afford club fees, so talent — not money — decides who gets to play.

Golf days & events

Run the whole day from one place.

Charity days, corporate scrambles, club opens — ForeAi handles registration, scoring and the clubhouse screen so you can enjoy the day.

  • ✓Team & sponsor registration — online forms or bulk-import an emailed spreadsheet.
  • ✓Live scoring on players' phones — any player in the group can post.
  • ✓Big-screen leaderboard and a live course map for the clubhouse.
  • ✓Side games & fundraisers — nearest-the-pin, longest drive, 50/50 and more.
  • ✓Weather & lightning alerts for the venue, live on the board.

Our mission

Talent should decide who plays — not club fees.

Golf changes lives, but club membership puts it out of reach for most South African children. We're building school leagues and working with Handicap Network Africa to affiliate kids who can't afford the fees — giving them an official handicap, real competition, and a path into the game.
