Privacy Policy

Your privacy, in plain English

Effective 16 August 2026

This policy covers the ForeAi and ECS Golf Day mobile apps and the foreai.co.za website. We keep data collection to the minimum needed to run golf days and coach your game. We never sell your data, and we don't use it for advertising or tracking.

What the apps collect

When you join or run a golf day, the app sends a few things to our server so live scoring works across everyone's phones:

  • Your name — the display name you enter, shown on the leaderboard.
  • Your golf handicap and hole scores — so the live standings are accurate.
  • A player ID and device ID — so your scores attach to the right person across devices. The device ID is not an advertising identifier.

That's the complete list of what leaves your phone from the app.

What stays on your phone (not collected)

  • Location / GPS — used only on your device to show distances to the green and point you to the pin. It is never sent to our servers. If you use the on-course survey tool, the coordinates are saved on your phone and only shared if you choose to export them.
  • Camera and motion — the swing coach analyses your swing live on your device. Nothing is recorded, saved, or uploaded.

What the website collects

If you register a team, hole or prize on foreai.co.za, we collect the details you type into the form — typically a contact name, email address, phone number and your team or sponsor information — so the organisers can arrange the golf day and contact you about it. We use it only for that purpose.

How it's stored

Data is stored on our hosting providers' servers (the app backend and the foreai.co.za host) and kept only as long as needed to run the event and the app. You can ask us to see or delete your data at any time — just email us.

No accounts, no tracking

The apps have no login or user accounts. There are no analytics, advertising, or third-party tracking tools in the apps. We do not track you across other apps or websites.

Children

The apps are rated for general audiences and are not directed at children under 13. We don't knowingly collect data from children.

Changes

If we change this policy we'll update this page and the effective date above.
