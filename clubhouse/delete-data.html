How to delete your ForeAi data

The ForeAi app has no login or user account — a random device identifier stands in for one. You can ask us to delete the data linked to you at any time.

Request deletion

  1. Email support@foreai.co.za from any address.
  2. Put "Delete my data" in the subject.
  3. Tell us the name you played under and the golf day / event you joined (this is how we find your records, since there's no account).

We action deletion requests within 30 days and reply to confirm.

Email
support@foreai.co.za

What gets deleted

  • Your name, handicap and hole scores.
  • Any GPS position you shared during a live event.
  • The device/player link that identified this phone as you.

What may be kept

  • Subscription / purchase records held by Apple, Google Play and our payments processor (RevenueCat) are kept as required for billing, tax and fraud-prevention law, then deleted per their policies. See our privacy policy.

You can also ask an event organiser to remove you from a golf day directly, which deletes your player record for that event.
