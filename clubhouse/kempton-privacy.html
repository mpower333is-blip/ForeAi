App Privacy Policy

Your privacy, in plain English

Effective 17 September 2026

This policy covers the “Kempton Park Golf Club” mobile app for Android and iOS (package com.foreai.kempton), developed and published by ForeAi for Kempton Park Golf Club. We keep data collection to the minimum needed to run the club app. We never sell your data, and we don’t use it for advertising or tracking. The app has no login or password — linking your membership is optional.

What the app collects

You can use the GPS rangefinder, course maps and scorecard without giving us anything. If you link your club membership (optional), book a tee time, or enter a competition, the app sends the following to the club’s backend so those features work:

  • Name — shown on your digital membership card, the tee sheet and leaderboards.
  • Email address — to identify your membership and for booking/entry records.
  • Membership number (a user ID) — so your bookings, entries and account attach to the right member.
  • Golf handicap index — imported from Handicaps Network Africa (HNA) to show on your card and to calculate competition scores.
  • A device identifier and push-notification token — to bind your membership to your phone and to send club alerts. This is not an advertising identifier.

This data is optional — it is only collected once you choose to link your membership or use the member features. It is used only to run the club app (“app functionality”), is not shared with third parties, and is never used for tracking or advertising.

Location stays on your device

The GPS rangefinder uses your location on your phone only — to show distances to the green and your position on the course. Your location is never sent to or stored on the club’s servers.

Sensors used on your device

  • Camera — used on the device (for example to show your membership QR); nothing is recorded or uploaded.
  • Microphone & motion sensors — used on the device to detect a ball strike or swing for optional shot logging. Audio is analysed live and is never recorded, saved or uploaded.

Payments

Membership dues, green fees, competition entries and levies appear as invoices in the app. Where card payment is offered, it is handled entirely by PayFast on their secure checkout — we never see or store your card details. Otherwise you pay the club by EFT using the reference the app shows.

How it’s stored

Data is encrypted in transit (HTTPS) and stored on the club’s hosted backend, kept only as long as needed to run your membership and the app.

No tracking

There are no analytics, advertising or third-party tracking tools in the app. We do not track you across other apps or websites.

Request your data or deletion

You can ask to see or delete your data at any time — email golfforeai@gmail.com or the club at clubmanager@kemptongolfclub.co.za, and the club office can also remove your member record.

Children

The app is a members’ club utility intended for adults (18+) and is not directed at children. We don’t knowingly collect data from children.

Changes

If we change this policy we’ll update this page and the effective date above.
